Convert YouTube videos to MP3 or MP4

YT-Ai turns a YouTube link into an audio file (MP3) or a video file (MP4) that you can save to your phone or computer. There is nothing to install and no account to create. It works in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iOS.

How to convert a YouTube video in 3 steps
