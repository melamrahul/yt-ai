Frequently Asked Questions

What resolutions can I download?

From 360p up to 2160p (4K), depending on what the original YouTube video was uploaded in. If the video only offers 720p, selecting 1080p will still return the best available.

Why does my download stay at a lower resolution?

YouTube compresses videos based on the uploader's settings. A 720p source cannot produce a true 1080p or 4K file — the converter returns the best quality actually available.

Does it work for YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Shorts download as vertical MP4 files. Paste the Shorts URL just like a normal video link.

Where does the file save on iPhone?

In Safari, tap the download icon in the address bar, then open the Files app → Downloads. You can move it to Photos if it's a video.

Is it legal?

Only download videos you own or those licensed for reuse (public domain, Creative Commons). Downloading copyrighted content may violate YouTube's Terms of Service.
