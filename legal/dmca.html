Our Position

YT-Ai is a passive technical tool that converts publicly available YouTube URLs into audio files at the request of the user. We do not host, store, or distribute any copyrighted content. Files are processed transiently and deleted after download.

All content accessed through the Service originates from YouTube and remains subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and the rights of its respective copyright holders.

Filing a DMCA Notice

If you believe your copyrighted work has been made available through the Service in a way that constitutes copyright infringement, you may submit a written notice to our designated agent. Your notice must include:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorised to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, including the specific URL(s) on YT-Ai where the material is located.
  4. Your contact information (address, telephone number, email).
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf.

Important: Because YT-Ai does not host content, notices about specific videos should generally be directed to YouTube itself. However, we will still review requests to block specific URLs from our converter.

Where to Send

Submit DMCA notices via our contact form with the topic "Copyright / DMCA". Include all required information from the list above.

Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was blocked in error, you may submit a counter-notice with the same contact form. We will review it and may restore access if appropriate.

Repeat Infringers

We may block URLs and IP addresses associated with repeated infringement claims.

Misrepresentation Warning

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages, including attorney fees.

YT-Ai is intended for personal, fair-use archiving of content you own or that is licensed for reuse (public domain, Creative Commons). Users are solely responsible for ensuring their use complies with copyright law.
