Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Overview

YT-Ai ("we", "us") provides a free online YouTube to MP3 converter. This policy explains what data we collect, how it's used, and your rights. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not store personal details.

What We Collect

  • YouTube URLs you paste — sent to our conversion server to fetch the audio. They are not stored after conversion.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and device information, collected by our analytics and ad partners.
  • Voluntary information — if you contact us via the form, we receive your name, email, and message.

How We Use It

  • To process your conversion request and return the MP3 file.
  • To prevent abuse (rate limiting, captcha verification).
  • To improve the service (aggregate usage statistics).
  • To respond to your contact form enquiries.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third parties, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile — bot protection on the converter.
  • Google AdSense / ad networks — display advertising. These may set cookies.
  • Analytics — aggregated, non-identifying usage data.
  • Web3Forms — processes contact form submissions.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for theme preference (light/dark) and language. Ad partners may set their own cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work.

Data Retention

Converted files are deleted from our servers after download. URLs are not logged. Contact form submissions are kept only as long as needed to respond, then deleted.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us via the contact form to exercise these rights.

Children

YT-Ai is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
