Frequently Asked Questions

What audio formats are supported?

Currently only MP3. It's the most universally compatible audio format — plays on every device, car stereo, and app.

What's the difference between this page and the main converter?

None functionally — both extract MP3 audio. This page is optimized for audio-focused users who arrive from search results about "YouTube to audio" or "extract audio from YouTube".

Can I extract audio from a YouTube Short?

Yes. Paste the Shorts URL just like a normal video. Audio extraction works the same way.

Why does the file sound different from YouTube?

YouTube compresses audio on upload. Converting to MP3 adds one more compression pass. Using 320kbps minimizes quality loss, but the result can never exceed the source quality.

Is downloading audio legal?

Only for content you own or that is licensed for reuse (public domain, Creative Commons). Downloading copyrighted music may violate YouTube's Terms of Service and local copyright law.
